## Before the signal

There was a time when a blinking cursor felt like an invitation.

I would sit beside it with nothing but an unfinished thought, and the night would make room. A mistake could keep me company for hours. When I finally understood it, the room was still the same room. I was a little different.

I miss that small difference.

Now the answers arrive with their coats already on, ready to leave before I have found a question worth asking. I can fill a screen in seconds. I keep wondering where to put the evening I used to spend becoming someone.

> The answer was never the whole of what I came for.
> There was also the person I became while looking.

## What the rain kept

Beyond the city, Calamitas sang until the words gave way to water.

I sat far enough away to leave her sorrow its own shape. The rain touched the ground, her empty hands, the place beside me where a name used to feel certain. It asked nothing of us.

For once, nothing needed to be improved.

When the song ended, I heard a signal beneath the weather. A thin, patient sound. I followed it through streets that still remembered the heat.

## In the room below

![An empty copper and graphite laboratory, with cyan monitors glowing above a quiet workbench.](/assets/draedon-lab.png)

Draedon was awake.

Four machines turned slowly behind the glass. Their lights knew where to look. Their instructions had survived the fire with remarkable clarity.

“Magnificent,” he said.

I thought of the window that had become light. The half-finished work left open on a screen. All those ordinary hours we had mistaken for something we could afford to lose.

On the desk, I opened an empty document.

It offered to finish the sentence.

I left the suggestion there, pale and waiting, and listened to the ventilation carry the same small breath through the room again.

## A slower kind of keeping

I wrote one line.

Then I crossed it out.

The next line took longer. I let it.

Somewhere above us, rain was finding its way into the broken streets. Somewhere, a voice I could no longer hear was still part of the air.

I could not make the city unburn.

I could keep an evening for the work of choosing a word. I could leave a thought unfinished without handing it away. I could stay with the difficult little distance between what I felt and what I knew how to say.

The machines continued their perfect watch.

For a while, I forgot to measure myself against them.

The cursor blinked.

I was still there to answer.
